Welcome to Whitecourt!

There's many unqiue things to do here!

Whitecourt's most notable attraction is the river slides in Rotary Park. They are fed by the lake with a water pump, and are rubberized so you can tube down it like a small river. Rotary Park also has a playground, splash pad, and pavilion, making it a hub for a lot of outdoor community events. For indoor community events, there is the Allen and Jean Millar center - Whitecourt's athletics and leisure center and swimming pool. Summer isn't the only time you can do things in Whitecourt, however! There is also Eastlink park, the local ski hill. It also boasts snow tubing. Of course, you can't forget the various walking, ATV and snowmobiling trails all around the town. All in all there are plenty of outdoor attractions. Whitecourt also has a wonderful mueseum known as the Forest Interpretive Center you can larn a lot of the town's history from.
