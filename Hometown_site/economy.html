Welcome to Whitecourt!

What oppertunites could Whitecourt have for you?

The town is notable for being within close distance of three separate mills and a paper plant, which of course is Whitecourt's main source of income given Millar Western is right in town and Canfor and Blueridge Lumber are very close by. Forestry is a huge industry as Whitecourt is simply surrounded by forests. Whitecourt also has a thriving craft community, hosted in year round markets such as White Wall Wood. There are also farmers markets hosted every Tuesday throughout summer, and also periodically through winter depending on vendor availability. Even the chain stores such as IGA will carry local products, making sure the community is also well supported. Of course, the most favoured stores, such as the butcher shop, are always local favourites and are preferred by the community.
