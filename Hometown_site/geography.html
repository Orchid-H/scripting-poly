Welcome to Whitecourt!

Admire the beauty of nature!

Whitecourt is divided into roughly two sections by the large hill the town is centered around. The way up the hill is lined with trees that are beautiful in autumn, and the forests are full of walking trails to follow. There’s also the Macleod river that runs near the back edge of the town which is a popular spot for boating and fishing. In addition to these other outdoor areas, there is also Rotary Park which boasts Whitecourt’s famous river slides in the summer, as well as a large lake that is stocked with fish that you can also tube on. There’s only a small portion of the town that is on the floodplain so there’s little risk in that way, and there’s something about the town that seems to avoid wildfires despite how heavily forested the area is, so damage risk is very minimal. Storms also don’t hit the area all too hard, generally, but winters are harsh as is common in northern Alberta.
