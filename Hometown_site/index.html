Welcome to Whitecourt!

A quaint town settled right in Canada's boreal forest!

Whitecourt is a small town in Northern Alberta settled in the boreal forest region of Canada. According to the official census of Alberta from 2025, Whitecourt currently has approximately 10 711 residents. Whitecourt also boasts three paper mills - The most notable being Millar Western, which is located right in town - which employ most of the people who live in the community and also sponsors several public spaces such as the Allen and Jean Millar Centre. Since Whitecourt is surrounded by forests there are many hiking trails which are stunning in the fall, and in the summer there are other river related amenities. There’s also plenty of chances for start ups in Whitecourt and there are a lot of small businesses that also support their communities. The community is mostly made up of workers and their families and there are full tracks for both public and Christian schools for said children.
