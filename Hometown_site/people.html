Welcome to Whitecourt!

Let's talk about the people!

The people who live in Whitecourt tend to be mill workers and their families, so the population is generally middle aged (according to census data from 2014 the average age is 35.7) with 0-14 year olds following up close after. This does mean there are many social events such as family nights and park barbeques geared at families primarily. This demographic is funnily enough also reflected in the fact the community centre is in the elementary school. In general, the people in Whitecourt are pretty normal and not unfriendly at all, but in general people who work together tend to form their own social groups so a better way to make friends is to join circles rather than letting a circle come to you.
