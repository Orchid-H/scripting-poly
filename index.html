Who is Orchid?

Orchid is a student at Lethbridge Polytechnic and is taking the Multimedia course. They are currently in their first year, and they are currently learning how to code, basic graphic design, resrech and writing, and digital audio and video. They are always on time and focused when it comes to a task they have to do, and they are a great problem solver.

What can Orchid do for you?

Orchid is a freelance writer in their spare time, specializing in fiction. They have been writing their own stories from a very young age and has been giving glowing reviews by their peers for their work. They have tried to get better at drawing but currently only really do picture editing for themself and others.

Orchid currently does writing requests for other people! They also dabble in video editing in their free time and enjoys getting requests from other people. You can also count on Orchid to do clear public speaking, and they have been in four independantly written plays so they have lots of acting experience.

Want To Know More?

Orchid currently lives alone but was helped with their move a lot by their older brother (pictured), who is the only family they have around as their parents still live in Northern Alberta, their grandparents and uncles and their families live in Ontario, and the rest of Orchid’s family live overseas in Britain. They moved for school and are trying to make new friends.

They haven't made anything published yet, but Orchid has some scraps of scripts prepared for a personal movie project. They have also made the barebones of some websites for a fan project they have been planning for a series they like! Orchid also dabbles in cosplay and makeup artistry in their spare time. If you're reading this you can probably infer Orchid has aspirations of greatness.
